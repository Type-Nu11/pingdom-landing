## 최종 생성 프롬프트

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: a single editorial photographic spread for a Korean local-discovery service landing page, 16:9 wide landscape.
Primary request: A credible, beautifully photographed Seoul neighborhood street, seen from behind a traveler who is discovering the local shops while walking. Early autumn, late afternoon approaching evening. A human-scale neighborhood alley with small independently operated restaurants and modest shops, lived-in architecture and realistic sidewalk texture.
Subject: One small traveler walking away from the camera, with at most one additional distant pedestrian. Position the traveler around 62 percent from the left, occupying only a small part of the frame. Casual clothing with a small shoulder bag. This is a candid walk through the city, not a posed tourism advertisement.
Composition/framing: A wide, calm cinematic photographer's view from standing eye level, realistic lens perspective and strong street depth. Buildings and street textures are the main subject; the small figure gives scale. Maintain believable natural proportions, no giant foreground subject. Frame the traveler and interesting shop frontage in the right-center so a thoughtful portrait crop around them remains possible. Preserve a generous left portion of the city scene. The frame is one continuous photograph, never a collage.
Lighting/mood: Natural subtle warm-neutral western afternoon sunlight across charcoal-gray city surfaces, just before dusk. Soft realistic shadows, pleasant but understated. Shop interiors may have a little warm everyday light. The sky is a restrained pale gray-blue, not a saturated dramatic sunset.
Materials/textures: Believable weathered brick, painted concrete, small glass-fronted shops, worn pavement, drains, doors and quiet everyday urban details. Editorial photo detail, natural imperfect surfaces, believable edges and human anatomy.
Color palette: Warm neutral stone and muted wood, urban charcoal, subtle early autumn greenery. No brand-pink light or tinted wash.
Constraints: Do not include an identifiable real brand or logo, do not create app interfaces or UI, do not add captions or typography. Store signs should be small blank/abstract or naturally out of focus with no readable generated letters. This is an imagined brand atmosphere image, not evidence of a specific real service location.
Avoid: glossy CGI, hyper-polished concept art, ornamental architecture, luxury resort appearance, advertising exaggeration, pink neon, magenta lighting, glowing sky, unnatural bloom, lens flares, random shapes, decorative graphics, collage, duplicate people, crowd, large close-up person, watermarks.
